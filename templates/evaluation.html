Metric definitions

  • RMSE / MAE - average gap between predicted and actual star rating.
  • Precision@K - share of the K recommended movies the user actually liked.
  • Recall@K - share of all liked movies that appear in the top K.
  • NDCG@K - ranking quality that rewards putting liked movies near the top.
  • Coverage - share of predictions the algorithm could make itself (rating table) or share of the catalogue it ever recommends (ranking table).
